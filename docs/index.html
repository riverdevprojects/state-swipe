Stateswipe support

Stateswipe is an offline trivia game for iPhone. Guess the U.S. state from up to five hints.

How do I play?

Tap the question mark in the top-right corner of the game for a guided practice round. Animated hands show which controls to tap. Practice does not change your current game or personal best.

How does scoring work?

Each state starts at 1,000 points. The first hint is free. Additional hints deduct their displayed cost. A correct guess earns the remaining points; an incorrect guess earns zero. You have one final guess per state.

What can I enter?

Type the full state name or its two-letter postal abbreviation. Small, unambiguous spelling errors are corrected. Text that cannot be matched to a state does not consume a guess.

Can I change the number of rounds?

Tap the sliders button and choose 1–50 rounds. Starting a new session replaces the current one. A separate personal best is saved for every session length.

Where is my progress saved?

Current games and personal bests are stored on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes its local data. Offloading the app preserves its data. Device backups may include game data according to your Apple settings. Stateswipe has no account or separate cloud-sync service.

Which iPhones are supported?

Stateswipe requires iOS 16 or later, including iPhone 8 and iPhone 8 Plus running iOS 16.

Report a problem or ask a question

Open a support request on GitHub. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and what happened. GitHub requests are public: do not include passwords or other private information.

For privacy questions, use the same support link. Read the privacy policy for details on data handling.
