Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This policy describes the Stateswipe iPhone app and its support pages.

Information collected by the app

Stateswipe does not collect, transmit, or sell personal information. It does not use advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, accounts, or third-party SDKs. All gameplay works offline.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your current game, hint rotation, and personal bests locally on your device. This information is used only to resume games, vary clues, and show your scores. It is not sent to the developer. Device backups may include this data according to your Apple settings.

Removing saved data

Deleting Stateswipe removes its local game data. Offloading the app preserves it. You can manage device backups through your Apple settings.

Support and this website

If you choose to contact support through GitHub, the information you submit is handled by GitHub and may be public. This is separate from using the app. These pages contain no analytics scripts or tracking cookies added by Stateswipe. Your hosting and service providers may process ordinary connection information under their own policies.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from users of any age.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated to describe them.

Contact

For questions about this policy, open a support request. Do not include sensitive personal information in public requests.
