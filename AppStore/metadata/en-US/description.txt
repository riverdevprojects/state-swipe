How well do you know the 50 U.S. states? Reveal clues, enter your guess, and see how many points you can keep.

Five hints per state
Start with a floral emblem, then uncover increasingly helpful clues about landmarks, capitals, and more. Your first hint is free. Every additional hint has a visible point cost.

One final guess
Type a state name or its two-letter abbreviation. Small, unambiguous spelling mistakes are corrected. Correct answers earn the points remaining; wrong answers reveal the state and earn zero.

Choose your session
Play anywhere from 1 to 50 rounds, with five rounds as the default. States do not repeat within a session, and each session length has its own personal best.

Learn by tapping
The interactive tutorial shows you where to tap, how points work, and how to submit a guess. Practice without changing your game or score.

Made for iPhone
Play offline, resume your current session, and keep personal bests on your device. Supports iPhone 8 and later running iOS 16 or later, with larger text, VoiceOver labels, and reduced-motion animations.

No accounts, ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.
