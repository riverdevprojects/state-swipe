# App Review notes

Stateswipe is an offline U.S. state trivia game. No login, paid features, advertisements, external services, or special reviewer access are required.

- Tap the question mark at the top right to open the guided practice tutorial. Tap the controls indicated by the animated hand. Practice is separate from the saved game; Close or Skip returns to the unchanged game.
- In the main game, tap hint cards in order. The first is free; later hints deduct their displayed point cost. Enter a state name or two-letter abbreviation and tap the orange arrow. A valid state guess is final; invalid or ambiguous text does not use the guess.
- Use the sliders button to choose 1–50 rounds. Personal bests are stored independently for each session length.
- Privacy information is available under Session settings → Privacy & app information.
- All content is bundled. UserDefaults stores only the current game and personal bests on the device. The app does not transmit data.

The app requires iOS 16.0 or later and targets iPhone. Its minimum deployment target is intentionally retained for iPhone 8 compatibility.
